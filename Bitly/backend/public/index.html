💡 System Design Interview Decisions (Evan's Deep Dives):

  • Why 302 Found instead of 301 Moved Permanently? 301 is aggressively cached by browsers and CDNs, which would blind the service from tracking analytics, user clicks, and enforcing expiration. 302 ensures every request hits our edge.
  • Why Sequential Counter + Feistel instead of Random MD5 Hash? Random hashing causes the Birthday Paradox collision rate (~880,000 collisions across 1B links), requiring expensive DB pre-checks. Sequential counters have 0% collision rate, and the Feistel cipher obfuscates them into unpredictable codes.
  • Why Batch Allocation? Write instances reserve chunks of 1,000 IDs via INCRBY. An instance only calls Redis once every 1,000 writes, enabling massive throughput.
